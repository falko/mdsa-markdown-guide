MDSA
:   Model Driven Specification Authoring: the OMG templates and tools for
    producing specification documents from LaTeX, Markdown and models.

Pandoc
:   Document converter that turns the Markdown files into LaTeX
    [@PANDOC].

pandoc-crossref
:   Pandoc filter that numbers figures, tables and sections and resolves
    references to them [@CROSSREF].

Identifier
:   Label of a section, figure or table, such as `sec:figures`, used to
    refer to it (see [Identifiers](08_Sections.md#identifiers)).
