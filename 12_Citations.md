# Citations

Cite a bibliography entry by its key:

| You write                  | Output                  |
|----------------------------|-------------------------|
| `[@DMN16]`                 | [@DMN16]                |
| `[@DMN16; @SCE10]`         | [@DMN16; @SCE10]        |
| `[@UML251]` (core entry)   | [@UML251]               |
| `[@PANDOC, Section 8]`     | [@PANDOC, Section 8]    |

Table: Citation syntax and output {#tbl:citations}

The keys come from `omg.bib`, `iso.bib` and `w3.bib` in `mdsa-omg-core`, and
from `specification.bib` in this repository. Cited entries appear in
Clause 3. Entries listed with `\addtocategory{normative}{...}` in
`_Specification_AuthorSettings.tex` appear under "Normative References", all
others under "Non-normative References".
